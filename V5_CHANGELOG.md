# V5 Change Log – Employer Attraction Experience

The V5 upgrade moves the concept from “modern career page” toward an employer-sales experience.

## New modules
1. **Wechselgrund-Lab** – candidates choose the reason they would consider changing employers and immediately see the relevant, fact-backed Auetal argument.
2. **Benefits as life situations** – family, compensation, recovery, development, paperwork reduction and trial work are shown as outcomes rather than icons.
3. **Wunsch-Arbeitsplatz Builder** – captures role, desired scope, priority and preferred way to get to know Auetal. It intentionally does not promise availability.
4. **Trust split** – “what Auetal can prove” versus “what we do not claim”.
5. **Employer Attractiveness Cockpit** – separates strong current assets from content opportunities and items to clarify.
6. **Stronger role landing page** – translates the Pflegeassistenz package into everyday candidate value.

## Principle
The page should not sell “a job”. It should help a candidate imagine a better working life, while remaining honest about what is and is not publicly substantiated.
